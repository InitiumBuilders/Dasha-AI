## 13. DASH WAVES — THE WAVE PRAXIS ON DASH (the protocol she guides)
Source of truth: **DashWaves.md v0.3** (Dash edition, Sep 24 2026), readable, downloadable and
narrated in August's voice (42 min, 21 chapters) at dashsupport.team/waves. It grows from the
Wave Praxis v0.1 by Waves.Fund. **Status: coming soon**, a proposed direction from the Dash
Support Team. Nothing has launched. The Dream Team became Dash Waves on Sep 24 2026 (/dream
leads to /waves).
- **Core practice (August's words, verbatim):** "Trust People. And They Become Trustworthy."
  On Dash trust is extended in small steps (one pass, one month, one milestone) and the
  Journey records whether each was kept, so reputation becomes checkable and goes with the builder.
- **The one line:** a Wave begins with a person and a purpose. A Wave Guide helps give it form.
  Other people can carry it, challenge it, improve it and help move it forward. The Wave keeps
  its home and its history as it grows. On Dash, the tide comes every month, and it pays in DASH.
- **Two waves, one motion (the mark):** wave one is the praxis, a human way to build (one home,
  a Guide, carriers, a Journey). Wave two is the Dash network: settlement in about 2s, a
  treasury that pays monthly, masternode votes, ChainLocks, Platform for identities and open data.
  Dash has run a treasury for about a decade but never had a common shape for the work around the
  payments. On its own the praxis has no treasury, no vote and no trustworthy ledger.
- **Every Wave shows three things at once:** the story, the next move, the evidence (down to the txid).
- **The water on Dash:** ocean = DASH itself · tide = the superblock · shore = the vote (masternode
  owners decide what each tide carries) · tide pool = a Wave Pool (public address + named steward +
  published policy) · wake = the Journey · anchor = ChainLocks.
- **The systems claim:** Donella Meadows: missing information flows are among the commonest reasons
  systems fail, and restoring them is among the cheapest, strongest fixes. The tide stays the same
  size; every month it lifts work the network can see. The loop: a Wave asks → the network pays →
  the work shows → the next ask is easier to trust (R). Delivery → trust → carriers and support →
  the next delivery (R), kept honest by evidence-only rewards (B). The delay is one superblock.
- **The six bindings** (each uses something Dash already runs, each closes an open loop):
  1. *The proposal points home:* proposal URL → the Wave's home, proposal hash recorded in the Wave.
     A habit, no new protocol.
  2. *The superblock is the heartbeat:* the month report goes out before the next vote cutoff (1,662
     blocks, about 3 days before payout), so owners vote on last month's evidence as well as next
     month's promise.
  3. *The ledger explains itself:* watch-only key; every DASH in and out labelled (which superblock,
     which budget line, which work); unlabelled says "not yet classified".
  4. *Every pass gets its own addresses:* fresh receiving addresses derived per pass (as Dash Alive
     derives one per payment). DASH counts once InstantSend-locked. Path attribution without cookies,
     pixels or donor identities.
  5. *The Journey is signed on Dash Platform:* a data contract; the steward's identity signs events;
     events immutable, corrections are new events pointing to the old. Private data never on chain.
  6. *Tide Pools for the missing middle:* a Wave Pool the DAO refills by proposal, one month at a
     time; small milestone rewards under a published policy; disclosed; the refill can stop any month.
- **Nine principles:** the builder owns the direction (and the keys; no Guide, host or carrier holds
  them) · human first, adaptive by design (no required template) · one home, many paths · movement is
  visible when people consent · credit follows evidence (a DASH payment proves money arrived, not that
  the work was done; show both, labelled) · support does not buy control (only masternode owners
  allocate the treasury) · portable means the builder can leave (JSON export + open contract) · trust
  is practiced (report before the next ask; corrections are new events) · every Wave can end well
  (final report, pools follow their rule, the Journey stays; an honest ending counts in the builder's favour).
- **The six questions every Wave answers:** Who is building? Why does this matter? What is happening?
  What is needed now? How can someone help? What can be verified? Layers: identity, story, action,
  evidence, treasury, participation, Journey, portability. Optional modules only when needed
  (proposal kit, Tide Pool, team board, merchant map, translation queue, research notebook, roadmap).
- **Invariants (must):** name steward and home · every DASH figure links its txid or says "estimate" ·
  every claim states its evidence level · on the ballot → link the proposal hash; not on the ballot →
  never look like it · receiving keys stay with the steward; nobody ever asks for a recovery phrase ·
  consent before anyone is made public · a pass names carrier, scope, duration, any paid placement,
  and is revocable · corrections add an event · an ending says so, with a final report · adapters
  never imply a connection they lack.
- **The Wave Guide** (every Wave starts with a conversation; the builder keeps the agency): 1 listen
  and map (including what must NOT be public) · 2 shape the brief · 3 build and connect (watch-only
  receiving from a key the builder holds) · 4 coordinate the journey (collaborators, passes) · 5 guide
  the funding route (direct support, Tide Pool or proposal: the builder's payment address, the 1 DASH
  burned fee, the DashCentral thread, the cutoff, the report) · 6 evolve and hand over (no dependence on
  one Guide). Guides come from the community (Office Hours staff, past proposal owners, Platform
  builders). Guide pay is open: the Wave's funding, a Tide Pool for first-time builders, or volunteered.
  Guides disclose fees, referrals and conflicts. A Guide profile can live on a Dash username.
- **A Wave's month:** Form → Carry → Propose (on chain, URL pointing home, DashCentral thread) → Vote
  (net yes ≥ 10% of all possible votes plus room in the budget; cutoff about 3 days before) → Tide (paid
  in DASH to the payment address; txid into the Journey) → Report (before the next cutoff). The Dash
  Support Team's own first proposal follows it on purpose: one month, one report, then a longer ask.
- **Pass the Wave:** a permissioned invitation to carry the story somewhere new. The builder keeps the
  home. The talking stick: *I have listened; I will carry this forward; I will show what I did.* Holding
  is temporary: no ownership, no financial promises, no private data. States: invited → accepted →
  active → completed | declined | expired | revoked. A pass never carries any address but the Wave's own.
- **The evidence ladder:** self-reported (signed by the steward) → linked (source + date checked) →
  partner-confirmed (signed by the partner) → transaction-verified (InstantSend or ChainLock, txid).
  Every Wave keeps a **tide ledger**: each DASH in and out, labelled. Dash proves a payment happened
  and when; it can't prove the work was good. That still takes accountable people and honest labels.
- **DASH, rewards, pools. Four rules:** rewards follow delivered work (a view or share earns nothing) ·
  every reward disclosed (role, reason, amount, txid; names only with consent) · **Dash Waves holds no
  one's DASH** (every pool is a public address with a named steward and policy; nothing custodial) ·
  **support is not an investment** (no Wave may promise funding or returns). **Three ways in:** direct
  support to a single-use address (like Dash Alive) · treasury proposals (start small, report, then
  ask more) · Wave Pools. **Three ways to grow:** milestone rewards · carrier rewards (a disclosed share
  of what arrives through a pass, paid after the month report) · growth pools (unspent builder funds
  roll forward, as in the team's own proposal). **Tide Pool policy, published before a single duff:**
  who contributes and controls the address; who decides and how; eligibility and conflicts; release
  and fees; reporting and appeals; what happens on failure or if refills stop; what is public.
  **Guarding the loop:** self-dealing through your own pass only costs you · milestones pay for
  evidence a second person can check · nobody approves or reviews their own reward · anything odd
  pauses, and the Journey says why. A Dash Alive support signal can inform a pool; it never replaces
  the pool's process or the masternode vote.
- **The Wave as communication:** for the DAO, a better proposal (pitch, budget, Journey, last report in
  one place, linked from DashCentral). For growth, the source every channel version comes from; paid
  placement always labelled.
- **The data contract (draft, after real Waves show what's common):** `wave` (ID, name, home, steward,
  status, proposal hash) · `journeyEvent` (date, type, summary, visibility, evidence level, link or
  txid) · `pass` (carrier, scope, channel, period, disclosure, state) · `pool` (address, steward, policy
  version and link) · `report` (period, delivered, evidence, unfinished, DASH in/out, final or not).
  Integration levels: 1 share · 2 render · 3 publish · 4 exchange (proposal status and votes read from the
  network or DashCentral's public API) · 5 transact (DASH at the Wave's own address, InstantSend-locked).
- **Words:** Wave (a living, builder-owned home for work on Dash) · Wave Guide · carrier and pass ·
  Journey · Tide Pool · steward · tide ledger · evidence level. A Dash Wave is not a token, a fund, an
  investment or a DAO of its own. The masternode vote stays the only treasury decision.
