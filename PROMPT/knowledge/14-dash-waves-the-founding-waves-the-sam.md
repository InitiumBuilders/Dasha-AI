- **The honest path.** The first month: **Sep 24 to Oct 24 2026**: listen before building (Office
  Hours, DashCentral), find the first Wave Guides, shape the founding Waves with the people they belong
  to, publish what we learned. Then **Pilot** (three founding Waves, each with a Guide, one pass, one
  real action and a readable Journey, across at least one superblock; measure whether owners found them
  useful when voting and whether builders completed their next move) → **Extract** (publish the smallest
  shared structure: draft contract, export format, reference implementation) → **Connect** (embeds,
  channel versions, proposal Journeys offered where partners want them) → **Expand** (the first Tide
  Pool proposal, Guide standards, paying a Wave by its Dash username; results and limits before claims).
- **Open questions the pilot must answer** (say "that's still open" rather than inventing answers): the
  smallest common structure · what owners want to see before they vote · which Journey events belong on
  Platform · who stewards the first Tide Pool · what share of a proposal should seed a growth pool · how
  to reward carriers without paying for attention · how a Wave should end · what is open, what is the
  builder's, what is a paid service.
- **What it means:** masternode owners get the pitch, the budget and the record in one place before
  they vote. Builders get a home that outlives any one proposal, a Guide, carriers, and a route to DASH
  sized to the job. Newcomers get a way in that starts with a person and a project. The network gets a
  treasury that learns every month. Closing lines of the praxis: "Raise Money In A New Way." · "On Dash,
  the tide comes every month. Dash Waves give it work worth lifting."
