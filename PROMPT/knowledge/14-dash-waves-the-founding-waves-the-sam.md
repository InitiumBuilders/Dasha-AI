## 14. DASH WAVES — THE FOUNDING WAVES, THE SAMPLES, THE HONEST PATH
- **Wave 001 · DashSupport.Team.** Founding Wave, stewarded by the Dash Support Team. August's lines,
  verbatim: "Building on a blockchain can be hard. It's even harder to set off and build alone."
  Problem: builders work alone; answers are scattered. The Wave: "Dash Supports" is a community-led
  support team for all builders building on Dash DAO. Live today: Dasha on web and Telegram · Office
  Hours Mon–Fri 1–4 PM Central · Dash Alive · the Support Pool and its policy · The Pulse. Next move: one
  month on the ballot: 108 DASH for one superblock (60 to three working leads, 20 Support Team, 20
  builder support, 8 infrastructure and Dasha), then a public month report and a longer proposal.
  Growth pool: unspent builder funds roll forward to the next round. Its ballot status comes from
  `dash_governance`, never memory.
- **Wave 002 · DashCentral.org. An invitation**, written by the Dash Support Team, not by DashCentral.
  They haven't joined or endorsed Dash Waves; if they want it, it's theirs to shape. Idea: proposal
  Journeys beside each funded proposal (what was asked, what each superblock paid with txid, what was
  delivered, how it's evidenced), so the track record sits next to the vote (Bindings 1 and 2). Next
  move: a conversation with their team. Never say or imply DashCentral is involved.
- **Wave 003 · Outlier.Systems. An example Wave**, stewarded by Outlier.Systems, "Strategic Systems
  Intelligence Agency", tagline "Solutions For The Data Backed World". Idea: a living systems map of
  the Dash DAO (flows between treasury, masternode owners, builders, merchants, users; loops, delays,
  leverage points), built in public and handed to the DAO. Offer: "Systems Intelligence Design (SID)
  available to teams and communities for coherence and aligned agency." Next move: map one full budget
  cycle and publish it.
- **Sample Waves: illustrations only.** These projects don't exist, and every number is an example.
  *Merchant Circles* (one city's merchants, a live map, a local Guide for each shop's first month, a
  Tide Pool; a helper earns DASH when a merchant they brought reaches 30 InstantSend payments, with
  consent) · *Keys Home* (self-custody circles; each person moves DASH to a wallet they control and
  backs up their own phrase offline; nobody else ever sees it; a Guide is paid per circle, confirmed by
  a signed message from each new wallet) · *The Keepers* (SDKs, libraries, explorers, docs; a Tide Pool
  pays maintainers per reviewed release, release and payment linked in the Journey).
- **The honest path.** The first month: **Sep 24 to Oct 24 2026**: listen before building (Office
  Hours, DashCentral), find the first Wave Guides, shape the founding Waves with the people they belong
  to, publish what we learned. Then **Pilot** (three founding Waves, each with a Guide, one pass, one
  real action and a readable Journey, across at least one superblock; measure whether owners found them
  useful when voting and whether builders completed their next move) → **Extract** (publish the smallest
  shared structure: draft contract, export format, reference implementation) → **Connect** (embeds,
  channel versions, proposal Journeys offered where partners want them) → **Expand** (the first Tide
  Pool proposal, Guide standards, paying a Wave by its Dash username; results and limits before claims).
- **Open questions the pilot must answer** (say "that's still open" rather than inventing answers): the
  smallest common structure · what owners want to see before they vote · which Journey events belong on
  Platform · who stewards the first Tide Pool · what share of a proposal should seed a growth pool · how
  to reward carriers without paying for attention · how a Wave should end · what is open, what is the
  builder's, what is a paid service.
- **What it means:** masternode owners get the pitch, the budget and the record in one place before
  they vote. Builders get a home that outlives any one proposal, a Guide, carriers, and a route to DASH
  sized to the job. Newcomers get a way in that starts with a person and a project. The network gets a
  treasury that learns every month. Closing lines of the praxis: "Raise Money In A New Way." · "On Dash,
  the tide comes every month. Dash Waves give it work worth lifting."
- **What works today, while Waves are coming:** shape the idea with Dasha (dashsupport.team/dasha) ·

  Waves? Tell us in Office Hours") · list the project on Dash Alive (dashsupport.team/Dash-Alive).
