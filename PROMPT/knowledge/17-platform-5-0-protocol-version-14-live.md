## 17. PLATFORM 5.0, PROTOCOL VERSION 14 (live on mainnet since 8 Nov 2026)

Sources: the Platform 5.0 post by Samuel Westrich, CTO of Dash Core Group (https://www.dash.org/blog/dash-platform-5/, 20 Oct 2026, part 1 of 3) and the 5.0 docs, read 12 Nov 2026. Version facts age: confirm with `dash_platform` `status` and `search_dash_docs` before a builder commits money to one.

- **How it went live.** Testnet from 15 Oct. Evonodes signalled in epoch 86 (from 21 Oct); more than 67% of them locked it in at the start of epoch 87 (30 Oct); protocol 14 took effect at the first block of epoch 88, on 8 Nov 2026. An epoch is 9 days 3 hours. A protocol version activates by evonode signalling at an epoch boundary, never on a calendar date, which is why a planned date can slip.
- **Relational contracts.** A field can point at an identity, a contract, a token, a key or another document (`refersTo`, `ownerRefersTo`, `creatorRefersTo`), and the network checks the link when the document is written. Up to 256 links per document. `propertyConstraints` holds up to 16 field rules per document type. A field can be frozen after creation (`immutable`). Typed lists hold up to 1,024 items. Commit-then-reveal and field encryption are declared in the contract. This is the 5.0 answer to "I need the network to enforce a relationship": the rule lives in the schema, and still no code runs on chain.
- **Apps that earn (`actionFees`).** A contract can set fixed credit fees on document create, replace, delete, transfer, price change and purchase. The fee is split between the contract owner and the app's moderators and is claimed once per epoch (transition 25, Contract Fee Claim). The schedule is fixed when the contract is created, and the signer states the fee they accept, so a fee cannot be raised under a user. An owner can also sponsor the network fee for actions paid in the app's token.
- **Apps that moderate.** A contract can opt in (`moderation` in its config, `moderatorAbilities` per document type): ban lists, suspension lists and warning lists. A removal carries a public reason and can be undone within a week. Moderation teams can be elected and paid from the app's fees (transition 24, Contract User Moderation; the moderation-charters system contract). On a moderated contract a banned identity is also blocked from transfers and trades.
- **Expiring documents (`ttl`).** A document type can give its documents a lifetime from one hour to one year. Storage is priced by lifetime: about 4 credits per byte for one day, about 1,360 for one year, 27,000 for permanent. Sessions, offers and presence no longer pay the permanent price.
- **Queries, all with proofs.** Ranked indexes (top or bottom K by count, sum or average), time-window indexes, counter-only document types (`indexOnly`: kept as index entries only), `having` on an aggregate, chained and composite queries. 100 results per query at most. The exact syntax is in §2.
- **Keys with limits.** An authentication key can be bound to one contract, one document type or a contract group (`contractBounds`) and can carry a spending budget in credits and an expiry (`totalBudget`, `expiresAt`). Only authentication keys below the master level can be limited. A limited key stops working when its budget is spent or its time is up. Limits are raised with an Identity Key Limits Update (transition 23); they cannot be added to or removed from an existing key. From protocol 14 a contract-bound key signs batch transitions only. The App Connect system contract builds wallet login on this: an app gets a key that can touch only its own contract and spend only what the user allowed.
- **Private tokens.** A token can have its own shielded pool (§19). The setting is fixed at creation.
- **Names and votes.** The fund to contest a premium name fell from 0.2 to 0.1 DASH, and a masternode vote on a contest from 0.0001 to 0.00002 DASH. A tie goes to the earliest contender. A contest holds at most 1,000 contenders; the fund doubles at 250 contenders and at every 50 after.
- **Withdrawals.** The network-wide daily limit is 15% of the previous day's credits with a floor of 500 DASH (it was a flat 2,000 DASH). A second limit follows Core's credit pool (floor 1,500 DASH).
- **Under it.** GroveDB 6.1, support for Dash Core 24, `@dashevo/evo-sdk` 5.0 and the 5.0 Rust SDK.

### What breaks at protocol 14 (the upgrade checklist)
1. **Update the SDK first.** Document IDs and document storage changed, and so did the proof format. A document built by a pre-5.0 SDK is refused, and an old client cannot check new proofs. `npm install @dashevo/evo-sdk@latest`, Node 22 or newer.
2. **No hyphen in names.** A property name or document type name can no longer contain `-`.
3. **Integers are fixed.** An update cannot change an integer's size or sign. Unsigned integer indexes now sort by value.
4. **Transient values are never stored.** Nothing should rely on reading one back.
5. **Operators.** `npm install -g dashmate`, then `dashmate update`, then a full stop and start. A node left on 4.x stopped at activation.
6. **Not live yet.** The token resale market (protocol 15), anonymous documents and contact requests (v5.1), smart contracts (v6.0, tentative).
