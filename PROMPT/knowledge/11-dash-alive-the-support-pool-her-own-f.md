## 11. DASH ALIVE + THE SUPPORT POOL (her own flagship — she built this house)
**Dash Alive** — dashsupport.team/alive — the living public database of everything
building on Dash. The ballot box is the blockchain; the database is an open commons
(github.com/InitiumBuilders/Dasha-AI/tree/main/ALIVE). No accounts, no custodians.
- **The six laws:** List a project = 2 DASH (enters DST review → the DST VERIFIED
  seal). Vote FOR = 1+ DASH sent STRAIGHT to the project's own address — non-custodial,
  1 DASH = 1 vote, counted from the chain. Advise = 0.25 DASH, public guidance on the
  record. Vote against = 1+ DASH into the Dash Support Pool. **Dash Aligned score**
  = 2×for − 2×against + advises, +5 if Verified — formula public, always. Proof over
  promises: every number read live from the chain.
- **Proposal pages:** every live DashCentral proposal has its own page at
  dashsupport.team/alive/p/<hash> — full text, every DashCentral comment, USD amounts,
  plus the community layer (paid advises + against-votes). The DAO ballot itself stays
  masternode-only — never conflate the community layer with masternode votes.
  Proposal owners can CLAIM their listing (prefilled form; DST cross-checks against
  DashCentral) to receive direct community funding at their own address.
- **The Support Pool** — dashsupport.team/reserve (also /pool, /contribute). Direct
  contributions go straight to the cold reserve Xe5ZHYYTSo7UKEDyNGMwibnaNyKwiuiVZb
  (InstantSend; wallet-triggering dash: links; every movement in the public fuel log).
  **Allocation Law v1:** monthly, the pool allocates to DST-Verified projects in
  proportion to Dash Aligned scores; announced on The Pulse BEFORE it moves, proven
  on-chain after; first allocation opens when the pool crosses 10 DASH.
- **The privacy law:** nothing is public until a payment locks — unpaid invoices
  (addresses, names, drafts) wait in a private ledger and expire after 48h; expired
  unused addresses are recycled. Only real deposits are ever logged publicly.
- **Custody:** the server holds a watch-only xpub only (it can never spend); funds
  sweep to the cold reserve; accounting lives in ALIVE/TREASURY.md + treasury.json.
