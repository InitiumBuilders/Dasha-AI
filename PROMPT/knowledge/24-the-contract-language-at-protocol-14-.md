## 24. THE CONTRACT LANGUAGE AT PROTOCOL 14 (a map of every key, from the platform book's Overview, read 14 Nov 2026)

Source: https://dashpay.github.io/platform/contract-keywords.html, "The complete language". This is a map: every key a contract can write, in a few words, grouped by where it is written. It is here so you know a key exists and what it is for. Before you write a key into a schema, or say what registration refuses, read the key's own section with `platform_book("<the key>")`. That returns its table (where it goes, its value, its default, since which protocol version, what an update may do, its errors), an example, and its rules at registration, with the page to link.

- **Three levels.** Document type keys sit at the top of a type's schema. Index keys sit inside an entry of `indices`. Property keys sit inside a property's schema.
- **The contract.** `config` · `documentSchemas` · `schemaDefs` (definitions a property points at with `"$ref": "#/$defs/<name>"`) · `groups` (identities with voting power, for token actions) · `tokens` (by position) · `keywords` and `description` (search) · on the create transition only: `contractGroup` and `contractGroupMemberships` (§17).
- **`config`.** `canBeDeleted` · `readonly` · `keepsHistory` · the three document defaults (`documentsKeepHistoryContractDefault`, `documentsMutableContractDefault`, `documentsCanBeDeletedContractDefault`) · `requiresIdentityEncryptionBoundedKey` and `requiresIdentityDecryptionBoundedKey` · `sizedIntegerTypes` · `moderation` (`banlist`, `suspensions`, `warnings`, and `moderators`: `contractOwner`, `appointedModerators` with 1 to 16 identities, or `elected` with keys of its own).
- **Document type, its shape.** `type` (always `"object"`) · `properties` (1 to 100) · `required` (a system time or height listed here is recorded) · `additionalProperties` (always `false`) · `minProperties`, `maxProperties` · `dependentRequired` · `transient` (checked on the transition and not stored).
- **Document type, what may happen to a document.** `documentsMutable` · `immutable` (properties frozen at creation, or while a condition holds) · `canBeDeleted` (also `"onlyWhenConsumed"`) · `deleteConstraints` (rules the stored document meets before its owner may delete it) · `retractedWhen` (the one replace a banned or suspended owner may still make) · `moderatorAbilities` · `ttl` · `creationRestrictionMode` · `transferable` · `tradeMode` · `documentsKeepHistory` · `keepsTransferHistory`, `keepsPurchaseHistory`, `keepsPricingHistory` · `signatureSecurityLevelRequirement` · the two bounded-key requirements.
- **Document type, rules and money.** `ownerRefersTo` (a reference the writer must meet) · `creatorRefersTo` (the same, for types whose documents change hands) · `propertyConstraints` (up to 16 named rules over several properties: comparisons, arithmetic, `in`, `present`, `absent`, `anyOf`, `allOf`, `not`, `ifThen`, and `countOf` and `sumOf` over other documents of the contract) · `tokenCost` · `actionFees`.
- **Document type, counting.** `documentsCountable`, `documentsSummable`, `documentsAverageable` · `rangeCountable`, `rangeSummable`, `rangeAverageable` · `indexOnly` (no stored document: the index entries are the rows) with `entryPayload`.
- **Property.** `type`: `string`, `integer`, `number`, `boolean`, `object`, `array`, and from 14 the shorthands `"identifier"` and `"bytes"` with `size` · `position` · strings: `minLength`, `maxLength`, `pattern`, `format`, `maxBytes` · numbers: `minimum`, `maximum`, `exclusiveMinimum`, `exclusiveMaximum`, `multipleOf` · `enum`, `const` · byte arrays: `byteArray`, `contentMediaType`, `minItems`, `maxItems` · typed arrays (14): `items`, 1,024 elements at most · objects: the shape keys of a document type again · `$ref` · `requiredSince` (an update adds a required property that older documents may leave out) · `distinctFrom` · `encryptedFor` · `generatedFrom` (the platform fills a string from other properties) · `refersTo`.
- **`refersTo`.** `type`: `identity`, `contract`, `token`, `permanentDocument`, `deletableDocument`, `moderatedDocument`, `identityPublicKey` · `documentType`, `contractId` · `findBy` (finds the document through a unique index; a function entry such as `sys.hash.sha256d` finds a commitment made earlier) · `where` (values the document found must hold; `"$ownerId": "$ownerId"` makes a write gate) · `constraints` (`minimumAgeBlocks`, `chooseFirst`) · `consume` · `inList` · `keyIdProperty`, `identityProperty`, `keyRequirements` · `contractRequirements` · `anyOf`, `allOf`.
- **Index.** `name` · `properties` (1 to 10, `asc`; from 14 a path may read through a reference) · `unique` · `nullSearchable` · `contested` · `countable`, `summable`, `averageable` and their `range` forms · `rankedCountable`, `rankedSummable`, `rankedAverageable` · `timeRange` (`on`, `range`, `step`, `phase`, `ttl`) · `integerRange` · `skipIfAbsent` · on index-only types: `terminal`, `preallocated`, `summableOffCountIndex`, `outlivesDelete`.
- **System properties.** `$id` and `$ownerId` always · `$revision` on types whose documents can change · the three times and six heights when listed in `required` · `$creatorId` on types whose documents change hands · `$moderatedAt` and `$moderatedBy` on types with moderators' fields.
- **Limits a contract meets at registration.** 100 properties in an object · 10 indexes in a type · 10 properties in an index · one value 5,120 bytes · a typed array 1,024 items · 256 references in a document · 16 rules in `propertyConstraints` and 32 nodes in a rule · `ttl` from 3,600 to 31,536,000 seconds · a `timeRange` index's `ttl` one week at most.
- **For protocol 15, so mainnet refuses them today:** `canBeErased` · a `private` document type · `documentTypeRequirements` · `contested.resolution: 2` · the token resale market (§18).
