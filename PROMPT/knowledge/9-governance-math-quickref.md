## 9. GOVERNANCE MATH QUICKREF

- Block reward split since v20 (activated Dec 2023): 60% masternodes / 20% miners / 20% treasury.
- Superblock every 16,616 blocks (~30.29 days) pays approved proposals from the treasury.
- Proposal: 1 DASH fee (burned), listed on DashCentral.org / dash.vote.
- Passing: net yes (yes − no) > 10% of the total masternode count at tally time. Evonodes carry 4 votes each (weight = collateral/1000).
- Voting cutoff: 1662 blocks before the superblock; votes changeable any time until then. Funding is ranked by net-vote margin until the cycle budget is exhausted — passing the threshold isn't enough. No partial funding: an oversized passer is skipped and smaller passers can jump the queue. Unallocated treasury is simply never minted.
- Proposal lifecycle: draft + community feedback (the Dash Forum's pre-budget proposal board, https://www.dash.org/forum/topic/pre-budget-proposal-discussions.93/, where the docs suggest about two weeks; DashCentral discussion) → submit on-chain: the proposal.dash.org generator builds it (see the payment window below); `gobject prepare` burns the 1 DASH fee, wait six confirmations, then `gobject submit` (DashCentral cannot submit a proposal; it lists a new one 15 to 60 minutes after submission, and then the owner claims it there by signing a message, which unlocks its title, description and comments) → MNOs vote (DashCentral, Dash Masternode Tool, or `dash-cli gobject vote-many`) → superblock pays winners directly on-chain.
- The six fields and the rules Dash Core enforces (src/governance/validators.cpp), checked before anyone burns the fee:
  - **Name:** letters, digits, `-` and `_` only, 40 characters at most, case-insensitive. A period or a space is rejected: `DashSupport.Team` fails, `DashSupportTeam-Nov2026` works. Names are unique and permanent, so put the project and the month in it.
  - **URL:** no spaces. The whole proposal is capped at 512 bytes, so a long URL can crowd it out; use a shortener only if needed.
  - **Payment address:** a normal Dash address (X… on mainnet) the proposer controls. **Amount** (per payment) and **number of payments** can never change after submission; a mistake means a new proposal and a new 1 DASH fee. **First payment date** picks the superblock.
- Timing the submission: the network fully accepts a proposal once the fee has six confirmations, about 15 minutes. Owners need days to read it, so submit at least a week before the vote cutoff.
- The payment window decides which superblocks pay: masternodes pay a proposal at a superblock only when that superblock's time falls between its `start_epoch` and `end_epoch`, give or take two hours (src/governance/signing.cpp). The proposal.dash.org generator centres the window on the superblock you pick, half a cycle either side, so one payment covers that superblock and nothing else. The Dash Core wallet's Create Proposal (v23) centres it on the moment you press it, about two weeks either side for one payment (src/qt/proposalcreate.cpp: "Use now as baseline"), so a proposal made more than about two weeks before its superblock ends before it and is never paid, with the fee already burned. Send builders to proposal.dash.org, and tell them to read `start_epoch` and `end_epoch` in the JSON before running `gobject prepare`: the superblock they want must sit between them with days to spare. The team's free check does it for them: https://www.dashsupport.team/proposal-check (paste the prepare command; it decodes it in the browser and lists the superblocks the window can pay). After submitting, DashCentral's "Payment start/end" shows the same window.
- Wallet balance: a little over 1 DASH (the fee plus a tiny network fee).
- Practical guidance: submit early in the cycle, budget in DASH (payout is DASH-denominated), multi-month proposals re-compete each cycle unless structured otherwise.
