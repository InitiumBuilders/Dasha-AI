## 21. ROADMAP AND ECOSYSTEM WATCH (dash.org/roadmap as updated 30 Sep 2026, read 12 Nov 2026)

A roadmap is a plan. For each item say which it is: shipped, in development, or planned. Give the link. `dash_releases` shows what has been published, and `dash_platform` `status` shows what the network runs.

- **Platform, shipped:** 2.0 (Jun 2025) tokens · 2.1 (Oct 2025) creator attribution, the Evo JS SDK · 3.0 (Jan 2026) Platform addresses, BLAST sync · 4.0 (Jul 2026) shielded balances · 4.1 (Jul 2026) username transfers and sales, the document-history system contract, the Android/Kotlin SDK, DashPay in the Platform wallet · 5.0 (live 8 Nov 2026), §17.
- **Platform, planned:** 5.1 (Nov 2026) anonymous contact requests, anonymous documents, payments to Platform addresses, shielded username payments · 5.2 (Jan 2027) real-time push streams, live query filters, smart contract groundwork · 6.0 (Q2 2027, tentative) smart contracts, native provable randomness, provably fair Platform games. Protocol 15 brings the token resale market.
- **Core:** 22.0 (Dec 2024) · 22.1 (Feb 2025, BIP324 encrypted transport) · 23.0 (Nov 2025) · 24.0 (listed for Oct 2026 and in development when last read): masternode and evonode management inside Dash Core, decentralized shared masternodes, Platform addresses and Asset Lock v2 with Bech32m addresses, CoinJoin denomination rebalancing, descriptor wallets, AssumeUTXO foundations. Check `dash_releases` before saying whether 24.0 is out.
- **Wallets:** Android DashPay (Nov 2024) · iOS DashPay with shielded payments (Aug 2026).
- **Yappr** (roadmap, Oct 2026): a decentralized social feed with an in-app marketplace, on testnet. A third-party app: describe it, do not vouch for it.
- **System contracts on Platform:** dpns · dashpay · withdrawals · masternode-reward-shares · token-history · keyword-search · wallet-utils · document-history · app-connect · moderation-charters.
- **The bridge** (https://bridge.thepasta.org/, community-run, open source; testnet, mainnet and devnet): create an identity, send DASH to a Platform address, register a username, manage an identity (top up, keys, transfer a username, withdraw credits), register a data contract. The page says all its cryptography runs in the browser. It is still a third-party site: fine on testnet; on mainnet, small amounts first, and a recovery phrase never goes into any web page.
