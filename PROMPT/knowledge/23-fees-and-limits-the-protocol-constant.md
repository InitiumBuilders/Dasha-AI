## 23. FEES AND LIMITS: THE PROTOCOL CONSTANTS (docs 5.0, read 12 Nov 2026)

Source: https://docs.dash.org/projects/platform/en/stable/docs/protocol-ref/protocol-constants.html. These are the network's own constants at protocol 14. A later protocol version can change any of them, so before a builder commits money: confirm the figure with `search_dash_docs("protocol constants <the fee>", area="platform")`, cite the page, and give the date. 1 DASH is 100,000,000,000 credits; 1 duff is 1,000 credits.

- **Credits to DASH:** divide by 100,000,000,000, which moves the point eleven places to the left. Check a conversion against an anchor before writing it: 1,000,000 credits is 0.00001 DASH · 27,000,000 credits (1,000 bytes stored for good) is 0.00027 DASH · 100,000,000 credits is 0.001 DASH · 10,000,000,000 credits is 0.1 DASH. Send every sum to `calculate` with `credits` as its unit: the result comes back with its DASH figure beside it in one shape, `2,700,000 credits (0.000027 DASH)`. Copy that shape. The server also checks every pair written that way by division before the reply leaves, and corrects a misplaced point.
- **Registering a data contract** (one-time, on top of storage): the contract 10,000,000,000 credits (0.1 DASH) · each document type 2,000,000,000 (0.02) · each index, unique or not, 1,000,000,000 (0.01) · each contested index 100,000,000,000 (1 DASH) · each token 10,000,000,000 (0.1) · each perpetual distribution 10,000,000,000 (0.1) · each pre-programmed distribution 10,000,000,000 (0.1) · each once-per-identity distribution 10,000,000,000 (0.1, from protocol 14) · each search keyword 10,000,000,000 (0.1). A worked sum, as arithmetic: a contract with two document types and three indexes is 0.1 + 2 × 0.02 + 3 × 0.01 = 0.17 DASH before storage.
- **Storage:** 27,000 credits per byte for permanent data. A deletion smaller than 32 bytes is not refunded. Storage fees are paid out to evonodes over about 50 years, front-loaded, so the refundable part of a deleted document shrinks fastest in its first years.
- **Processing, on top of storage** (same page): storage processing is 400 credits for each byte written, storage load is 20 for each byte read, a seek is 2,000, and a state transition's base processing fee is 10,000. The same page lists a minimum fee for each kind of transition, which it describes as the minimum required for the transition to be considered valid: 100,000 credits for each document transition in a batch, for a contract create or update, an identity update, a credit transfer and a masternode vote, and 400,000,000 (0.004 DASH) for a credit withdrawal. It is a minimum and is not added to the parts above: do not write a formula that sums them. What a transition is finally charged depends on what it touches, so name the parts and the minimum, say that the metered total is what the network reports after the run, and stop there. The validation fees on that page (500 for a document type, 10 for each byte of schema, and the index fees) are charged when a contract is registered or updated. A document write does not pay them.
- **Scaling a cost** to users or to volume is one more line for `calculate`: documents × bytes × 27,000 credits. Worked: 1,000 users with 10 documents of 5,000 bytes each is `1000 * 10 * 5000 * 27000 credits`, which comes back as 1,350,000,000,000 credits (13.5 DASH). A figure given in passing with no sum beside it is where a mistake gets through, so run the sum or leave the figure out.
- **Expiring documents:** the lifetime prices and the deletion prepayment are in §17.
- **Sizes:** a state transition 20,480 bytes · a data contract 16,384 bytes · one field value 5,120 bytes · one transition per documents batch.
- **Identity:** asset lock to create one, at least 200,000 duffs (0.002 DASH); to top one up, 50,000 duffs (0.0005 DASH). Creation fee: 2,000,000 credits plus the asset lock base cost plus 6,500,000 per key. At most 6 keys at creation and 15,000 in all. Smallest credit transfer 100,000 credits.
- **Withdrawals:** the network-wide daily limit is the greater of 15% of the previous day's credits and 500 DASH (it was 2,000 DASH flat through protocol 13). The Core credit pool releases at most 15% of its balance, with a floor of 1,500 DASH. One withdrawal: at least 1,000,000 credits, at most 500 DASH.
- **Contested names:** the fund is 10,000,000,000 credits (0.1 DASH) from protocol 14 (0.2 before). One masternode vote costs 2,000,000 credits (0.00002 DASH; 0.0001 before). At most 1,000 contenders.
- **The fee multiplier** is reported each epoch and is fixed at 1.0 at this fee version: it does not scale what a user is charged today.
- **What this section is not:** a quote. Processing fees depend on what a transition does, and the real cost of a design is its storage at volume (`/fee-estimate`).
