# DESIGNS — The Dash Design Library Commons

The open database behind [dashsupport.team/design](https://www.dashsupport.team/design).

## What lives here

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `community/index.json` | Every community-submitted design system — submissions, forks, votes, comments. The machine truth the Library renders. |
| `references.json` | The Constellation — curated design systems from across the product world that carry Dash's qualities. The one-line reads are our own, the previews are our own captures of the public product sites, and each entry links to the product itself. (Discovery for the initial curation was aided by public design galleries, incl. refero.design.) |

## How the community gallery works

- **Submit** a design system from the Library page — name, essence, palette, the system itself. It lands here, public, within minutes.
- **Fork** any design to evolve it — lineage is recorded (`forkedFrom`), credit flows both ways. (This folder lives in a public git repo: forking the whole commons is also just `git fork`.)
- **Vote** and **comment** on any design — one vote per browser, honest counts, no dark patterns.
- **Tips**: submitters may attach a Dash address or Evo username. If a design helps you, tip its designer directly — the money goes to them, never through us.
- **Share**: every design has a deep link (`/design#d=<id>`).

## The rules the commons keeps

- Everything here is public forever (MIT, like the rest of the repo) — submit only what you're glad to give the community.
- No impersonation, no stolen work: submit your own systems, fork with credit, cite what you reference.
- The team gardens the commons — spam and abuse get pruned; disagreement never does.
