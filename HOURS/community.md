# Help given

Anyone who helps a builder on Dash can log it, and is credited here. In the Dash Support Team's Telegram group, write `/helped 1.5 what you did`; anyone with a GitHub account can use the [Help given form](https://github.com/InitiumBuilders/Dasha-AI/issues/new?template=help-given.yml) instead, and those entries are the issues labelled `help-given`. Every entry is self-reported and links to where it was posted. This is credit, not pay: none of it is counted as the leads' hours or in the team's month report. A correction is a new entry with a negative number, never an edit. Totals: [The Pulse](https://www.dashsupport.team/pulse#helped).
